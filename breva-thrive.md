### Business Description
Music AI Lab is an edge AI and physical AI advisory firm providing fractional leadership and technical consulting to B2B and B2G clients. We specialize in deploying AI solutions at the edge — bringing intelligent computing closer to physical systems, sensors, and real-world applications where real-time, offline, or privacy-preserving AI is essential.

### How does your business create community impact?
Music AI Lab's work has direct community impact through the organizations we serve:
- **Public Safety:** We help government agencies deploy AI for real-time emergency response systems that operate even when internet connectivity is down
- **Environmental Monitoring:** Our edge AI expertise enables real-time environmental sensor networks that track air quality, water safety, and climate indicators
- **Infrastructure Protection:** We help organizations deploy AI for monitoring bridges, power grids, and water systems — critical infrastructure that protects communities
- **Democratizing AI Access:** Our fractional model makes world-class AI strategy accessible to smaller organizations and government entities that can't afford full-time AI leadership
