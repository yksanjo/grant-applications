**The Problem:**
AI is stuck in the cloud. Every breakthrough — from language models to computer vision — lives on massive servers halfway across the world. But the decisions that matter happen in the real world, in real time, often with no internet connection: a bridge detecting structural failure, a sensor catching a gas leak, a security system identifying a threat in milliseconds. These systems can't wait for a round-trip to AWS. They need intelligence at the edge. Now.

**The Solution:**
Music AI Lab builds EdgeDeploy — the pipeline that takes AI models trained in the cloud and compresses them for deployment on edge devices. We take the power of cloud AI and put it where it actually matters: on physical systems that operate autonomously, in real time, without connectivity.

**What We Do Today:**
- Already operating 3 years with ~$80K/yr revenue
- Serve B2B and B2G clients deploying AI in physical environments
- Expertise in model compression, edge optimization, and real-time inference
- Fractional AI leadership for organizations that can't afford full-time teams

**The Product Vision:**
EdgeDeploy as a platform: train models on AWS → auto-optimize for edge hardware → deploy to any device → monitor and retrain in real time. One pipeline. Any edge. Zero cloud dependency at inference time.

**Why $50K in AWS Resources Changes Everything:**

1. **EC2/GPU Instances** — train and test AI models before edge compression ($15K)

3. **IoT Greengrass** — edge deployment framework and monitoring ($10K)
4. **Lambda + API Gateway** — cloud-edge sync and retraining infrastructure ($10K)

With $50K in AWS, we transition from advisory services to building a scalable edge AI deployment platform — serving hundreds of organizations instead of a handful of clients.

**Why We Win:**
