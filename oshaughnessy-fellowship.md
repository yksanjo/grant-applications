## 6. Analysis of Failure

### What Failure Looks Like
Failure would mean remaining stuck in the advisory-only model — trading time for money without building scalable tools or frameworks. The business would plateau at its current revenue level, unable to serve more clients or create lasting impact. The edge AI space would be dominated by large consulting firms who productize what we know but package it for enterprises only — leaving smaller organizations behind.

### Potential Original Mistakes
- **Over-indexing on advisory:** Spending all time on client work without investing in product/tool development
- **Failing to open-source:** Keeping knowledge proprietary instead of building community and standards
- **Narrowing focus too early:** Picking one industry vertical before validating broader market demand
- **Underpricing expertise:** Charging too little for deep technical advisory, creating a sustainability ceiling
- **Not building in public:** Missing the opportunity to establish thought leadership and attract the right clients and collaborators
