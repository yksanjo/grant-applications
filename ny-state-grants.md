# NY State Grant Opportunities — Music AI Lab
**Location:** 515 E 14th St, New York 10009
**Status:** Research complete — some require LLC/corporation

---

## 1. NYSTAR Innovation Matching Grants
**Amount:** Up to $130,000
**Eligibility:** NY-based tech businesses with active federal SBIR/STTR awards
**Deadline:** Rolling (rounds open October and April)
**FREE:** Yes

**Status:** ⚠️ Requires active SBIR/STTR award first → Apply for NSF SBIR Phase I first, then NYSTAR kicks in as matching funds.

**Apply:** Empire State Development CFA portal at apps.cio.ny.gov

---

## 2. Empire State Development — Global NY Fund
**Amount:** Up to $25,000
**Eligibility:** Registered NY business (LLC, corp, or nonprofit)
**Deadline:** Rolling / periodically
**FREE:** Yes

**Status:** ⚠️ May require LLC or corporation — sole proprietorship may not qualify. Verify before applying.

**Apply:** apps.cio.ny.gov (CFA system)

---

## 3. Empire State Development — Regional Economic Development Council (REDC)
**Amount:** Varies ($60M total pool across all regions)
**Eligibility:** NY businesses aligned with regional economic priorities
**Deadline:** Typically late July (Consolidated Funding Application)
**FREE:** Yes

**Status:** ⚠️ Complex application, better for larger projects. Worth monitoring for future cycles.

---

## Strategy for NY Grants
1. **FIRST:** Register Music AI Lab as LLC (if not already) — unlocks state-level grants
2. **THEN:** Apply NSF SBIR Phase I (federal, up to $275K)
3. **AFTER:** Apply NYSTAR matching grant (up to $130K on top of SBIR)
4. **MONITOR:** Empire State Development CFA portal for open calls

---

## Notes
- Sole proprietorship limits NY state grant eligibility
- Consider incorporating as LLC to access full range of NY grants
- NY.gov Business Account required for all state applications
- Attend REDC workshops to align with regional scoring priorities
