### Business Description
Music AI Lab is an edge AI and physical AI advisory firm providing fractional leadership and technical consulting to B2B and B2G clients. We specialize in deploying AI solutions at the edge — bringing intelligent computing closer to physical systems, sensors, and real-world applications. Our work bridges the gap between AI research and practical, deployable solutions for government and enterprise clients.

### What problem does your business solve?
Organizations want to deploy AI in the physical world but lack the expertise to do so. Cloud-based AI doesn't work for real-time, low-latency, or offline applications. Music AI Lab bridges this gap by helping clients deploy AI at the edge — on devices, sensors, and physical systems where decisions need to happen in real time.

### How will the $2,500 grant and mentorship help your business?
The $2,500 grant would allow Music AI Lab to invest in edge AI development tools and hardware for testing physical AI deployments. The 2-month mentorship component is equally valuable — as a sole proprietor building a cutting-edge AI advisory practice, strategic guidance on scaling from micro-business to a sustainable consulting firm would accelerate our ability to serve more B2B and B2G clients.

### What makes your business unique?
We focus on the next frontier of AI: physical deployment. While most AI companies build cloud-based solutions, we help organizations deploy AI on edge devices, embedded systems, and autonomous platforms — where the real-world action happens. Our fractional model also makes world-class AI strategy accessible to organizations that can't afford full-time AI leadership.
