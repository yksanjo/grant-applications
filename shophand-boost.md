### What specific tech/operational challenge are you facing?
Music AI Lab needs to build a hardware testing lab for edge AI deployment — acquiring and configuring multiple edge computing platforms (NVIDIA Jetson, Google Coral, Raspberry Pi, custom ASICs) to test and validate AI model deployments across different hardware configurations. Currently, we advise clients on edge AI deployment but lack a dedicated testing environment to prototype and validate solutions before deployment. This limits our ability to serve clients efficiently and reduces the quality of our advisory services.

### How would the grant help your business?
Grant funding would directly fund the construction of an edge AI hardware testing lab, enabling Music AI Lab to:
1. Prototype and validate AI deployments across multiple hardware platforms
2. Reduce deployment risk for B2B/B2G clients
3. Develop standardized deployment templates that accelerate future projects
4. Create educational content and training materials for the community

### What technology or operational improvement are you seeking?
We need to transition from advisory-only to advisory + tooling. This means:
- Building a hardware lab for edge AI testing and validation
- Developing reusable deployment pipelines that work across hardware platforms
- Creating documentation and training materials that scale our expertise
- Implementing project management and client delivery systems to handle more concurrent engagements

### Expected impact of grant funding
- 50% increase in deployment speed for client projects
- Ability to serve 3-5 additional clients simultaneously
- Creation of open-source tools that benefit the broader edge AI community
- Establishment of Music AI Lab as a recognized authority in physical AI deployment
