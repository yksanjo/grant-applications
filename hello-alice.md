## Business Description

### What does your business do?
Music AI Lab helps organizations deploy artificial intelligence in the physical world — onto edge devices, sensors, and machines that need to make intelligent decisions in real time. We provide fractional AI leadership and technical consulting to B2B and B2G clients, bridging the gap between AI research and practical, deployable solutions for government agencies and enterprises.

### What problem are you solving?
AI models are powerful in the cloud, but many of the most important applications — defense, public safety, infrastructure monitoring, autonomous systems — need AI to work on physical devices, in real time, often without internet connectivity. Most organizations lack the expertise to make this happen. We solve that.

### What makes your business unique?
We focus on edge AI and physical AI deployment — the next frontier of artificial intelligence. While most AI companies build cloud services, we help organizations deploy AI where the real-world action happens: on devices, in machines, at the edge of the network. Our fractional model also makes world-class AI strategy accessible to organizations that can't afford full-time AI leadership.

### How will grant funding help your business?
Grant funding would allow Music AI Lab to invest in edge AI development tools, testing hardware, and open-source tool development. This would enable us to serve more B2B and B2G clients and create scalable resources that democratize access to physical AI deployment for organizations of all sizes.

### What are your business goals for the next 12 months?
1. Develop and release open-source edge AI deployment toolkit
2. Serve 20+ B2B/B2G clients through expanded advisory programs
3. Launch training and certification program for edge AI practitioners
4. Generate $200K+ in revenue
5. Establish Music AI Lab as recognized authority in physical AI deployment
