### Business Description
Music AI Lab is an edge AI and physical AI advisory firm providing fractional leadership and technical consulting to B2B and B2G clients. We specialize in deploying AI solutions at the edge — bringing intelligent computing closer to physical systems, sensors, and real-world applications where real-time, offline, or privacy-preserving AI is essential. Our work bridges the gap between AI research and practical, deployable solutions for government agencies and enterprises.

### What problem does your business solve?
Organizations want to deploy AI in the physical world but lack the expertise to do so. Cloud-based AI doesn't work for real-time, low-latency, or offline applications. Music AI Lab bridges this gap by helping clients deploy AI at the edge — on devices, sensors, and physical systems where decisions need to happen in real time. Our fractional model makes this expertise accessible to organizations that can't afford full-time AI leadership.

### How has your business adapted or overcome challenges in the past year?
As a sole proprietor, Music AI Lab has grown to ~$80K in annual revenue by focusing on a high-value niche — edge AI and physical AI deployment — that most consulting firms overlook. We've adapted to the rapidly changing AI landscape by staying at the cutting edge of edge computing technology, developing expertise in model optimization, deployment pipelines, and hardware platforms that most AI consultants don't understand. Our B2B and B2G client base has grown through word-of-mouth and demonstrated results.

### How would the $20,000 grant help your business?
The $20,000 grant would accelerate Music AI Lab's transition from pure advisory to advisory + tooling:
1. **Edge AI Hardware Lab ($10,000):** Acquire and configure multiple edge computing platforms for testing and validation
2. **Open-Source Tool Development ($5,000):** Invest in development of reusable deployment frameworks and documentation
3. **Business Infrastructure ($3,000):** Implement project management, CRM, and financial systems to scale operations
4. **Training & Community ($2,000):** Develop educational content and launch training programs for aspiring edge AI practitioners

This investment would enable Music AI Lab to serve more clients, create scalable resources, and establish itself as a recognized authority in physical AI deployment.

### What are your business goals for the next 3 years?
- Year 1: Develop open-source edge AI toolkit, serve 20+ clients, launch training programs, reach $200K+ revenue
- Year 2: Scale team to 3-5 contractors/employees, establish partnerships with edge hardware vendors, expand B2G contracts
- Year 3: Music AI Lab becomes the leading resource for physical AI deployment — setting standards and best practices for the industry
