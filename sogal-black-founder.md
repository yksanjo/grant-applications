### What does your business do?
Music AI Lab helps organizations deploy artificial intelligence in the physical world — onto edge devices, sensors, and machines that need to make intelligent decisions in real time without relying on cloud servers. We provide fractional AI leadership and technical consulting to B2B and B2G clients, bridging the gap between AI research and practical, deployable solutions for government agencies and enterprises.

### How will grant funding help your business?
Grant funding would allow Music AI Lab to build an edge AI hardware testing lab, develop open-source deployment tools, and scale our advisory practice to serve more organizations. This investment directly translates to better service delivery, more client deployments, and democratized access to physical AI expertise.

### What makes your business unique?
We focus on the next frontier of AI: physical deployment. While most AI companies build cloud-based solutions, we help organizations deploy AI on edge devices, embedded systems, and autonomous platforms — where the real-world action happens.
